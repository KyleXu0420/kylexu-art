My work philosophy

1. Think like water, act like jazz

Adapt fluidly to changing needs and embrace creativity and collaboration, like a jazz musician improvising and building on ideas.

2. Move fast, fail fast

Quickly prototype and iterate to learn from failures early, fostering innovation and aligning designs with user needs.

3. One step at a time

Approach design methodically, breaking it into manageable steps to ensure thorough refinement and maintain momentum towards user-centric solutions.
